Résumé

Ce texte part d'une question : depuis quelle échelle regardons-nous le vivant ? Il tient en une phrase, « le vivant est un recommencement à des échelles différentes », et la mesure en trois parties.

Chaque lecteur, de la bactérie à l'humain, lit dans une fenêtre de tailles, de fréquences et de rythmes, dont le seuil dessine une courbe en U.

Abstract

This text starts from a question: from which scale do we look at the living world? It holds in one sentence, "life is a recommencement at different scales", and measures it in three parts. Scale: every reader, from the bacterium to the human, reads within a window of sizes, frequencies and rhythms whose threshold draws a U-shaped curve. Perception: reading a trace requires an emitter, a carrier, a decoder and a shared scale; if a single piece is missing, the trace is there and no one reads it. Resonance: a system responds strongly to what falls on its frequency, and the same form returns from one level to the next. The pieces assemble into an equation proposed as a hypothesis: P(read) = Φ( √T · s · G ÷ √( σ²outside + (σinside ÷ G)² ) − criterion ), with G = carrier × decoder × scale(f). It builds on signal detection theory (Tanner and Swets, 1954), receiver noise (Friis, 1944) and the physical limit of detection (Berg and Purcell, 1977). Every claim carries its register (ESTABLISHED, DEBATED, HYPOTHESIS, READING, CALCULATION) and its source. 17 figures, more than 250 references. The text closes on a testable prediction. It continues The Diamond (Elias Sorel, 2026). Full text in French.
